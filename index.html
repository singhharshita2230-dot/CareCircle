YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

Your health details are private by default.

Doctors cannot open a patient’s medical reports or health history automatically. They must request access, and the patient stays in control at every step.

1

Doctor sends a health-access request.

2

Patient accepts, declines, or chooses what to share.

3

Patient can remove access at any time.
